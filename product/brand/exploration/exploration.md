## Preservation

- Boards retain their source dimensions and every decoded RGB and RGBA pixel through lossless WebP encoding.
- Every archived board is below 1,000,000 bytes; the legacy PNG is copied without modification.
- [Archive manifest](manifest.json) records native source filenames, source and archive hashes, and verification.
- Failed extraction and microedit outputs are excluded from this archive.
- The board artwork records exploration; it does not establish application integration.
